Privacy Policy for VaporNotes

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Overview

VaporNotes is a note-taking app that runs entirely on your device. This Privacy Policy explains what information VaporNotes handles, and — just as importantly — what it doesn't.

Information We Collect

VaporNotes does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

Specifically:

  • No account is required. There is no sign-up, login, email address, or personal identification of any kind.
  • No analytics or tracking. VaporNotes does not use any analytics service, advertising identifier, or usage-tracking tool.
  • No advertisements. VaporNotes contains no ad network and does not share data with advertisers.
  • No internet connection is required to use the app. VaporNotes works entirely offline.

Your Notes

All notes you create in VaporNotes — including their text, titles, tags, and timers — are stored only on your device, in a local, private database that other apps cannot access.

  • Notes are never uploaded, synced, or transmitted anywhere.
  • We do not have access to your notes. Nobody at VaporNotes can see, read, or recover them.
  • If you delete a note (or its timer expires and it fades away automatically), it is permanently removed from your device. We cannot restore it, because we never had a copy.
  • If you uninstall the app or lose your device, any notes stored on it will be lost, since no backup or cloud copy exists.

Please keep this in mind: because everything lives only on your device, protecting your device (with a screen lock, for example) is the best way to protect your notes.

Permissions

VaporNotes does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos, or any other sensitive device permission. The app only needs standard local storage access to save your notes on your own device.

Children's Privacy

VaporNotes does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information from anyone at all. The app contains no chat, social, or sharing features.

Changes to This Policy

If VaporNotes ever changes in a way that affects this policy — for example, if a future optional feature introduces cloud backup — this page will be updated first, and the update will clearly describe what changed and why. We will never introduce data collection silently.

If future versions of VaporNotes introduce additional features that require new permissions or data handling — such as an optional Private Vault or local reminder notifications — this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly to describe them before they are released.
