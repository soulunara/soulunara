Privacy Policy for VaporNotes

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Overview

VaporNotes is a minimalist note-taking app created by SoulUnara. The app is designed with privacy in mind and runs primarily on your device. This Privacy Policy explains what information VaporNotes handles and what it does not collect.

Information We Collect

VaporNotes does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information.

Specifically:

  • No account is required. There is no sign-up, login, email registration, or personal profile.
  • No analytics or tracking. VaporNotes does not use analytics services, advertising identifiers, or tracking tools.
  • No advertisements. VaporNotes does not contain advertising networks and does not share information with advertisers.
  • No cloud storage. Your notes are not uploaded to external servers.

Your Notes

All notes you create in VaporNotes, including text, titles, tags, and timers, are stored only on your device using local app storage.

  • Notes are never uploaded, synced, or transmitted anywhere.
  • SoulUnara does not have access to your notes and cannot read or recover them.
  • When a note expires or you delete it, it is permanently removed from your device.
  • If you uninstall the app or lose your device, your notes may be lost because no cloud backup exists.

Because your notes remain on your device, protecting your device with a secure screen lock is the best way to protect your information.

Permissions

VaporNotes does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos, or other sensitive personal data. The app uses local device storage to save your notes and provide its features.

Children's Privacy

VaporNotes does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because the app does not collect personal information from users. The app does not include chat, social, or sharing features.

Changes to This Policy

If VaporNotes introduces features that affect privacy, such as optional cloud backup or additional services, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are released.

We will clearly explain any changes to how information is handled.
